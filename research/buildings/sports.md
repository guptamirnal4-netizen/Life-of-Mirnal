- **Running track (OSM `track`, sport `running`):** a 21-point oval with bounds x 253…348, z 401…565, about 95 × 164 m. Its long axis runs about N–S, tilted about 10° clockwise like the ground [verified]. That is the right size for a 400 m track [approximation].
- **Inside the same ground (OSM):** two basketball pitches, one tennis pitch and the BITS Volleyball Court (see sections 2–4). Skating Area POI at (364, 375) [verified].
- **Unnamed OSM building:** x 235…269, z 335…361, h 7, kind `other`, at the ground's NW corner next to the basketball courts [verified]. It may be the indoor sports hall or the gym, but that is [unknown].
- **Neighbours:** Bhagirath Bhawan (232…299, 195…297) and Ashok Bhawan (309…376, 184…284) to the north. Shankar Bhawan (145…218, 345…455) and Shankar Vyas Mess to the west. Vyas Bhawan (181…246, 553…623) to the SW. Rakesh Verma Convention Centre (403…443, 313…353) to the NE. Faculty-housing-sized blocks to the east (x 450–580). Med-C ground to the SE [verified, data].

### B. Exterior (open ground)
- **Track surface:** unpaved, beige-brown sandy earth with faint white lane chalk. It is not a synthetic track [verified, Athletics panorama]. Hurdles and plyo boxes appear on it in the panorama [verified].
- **Infield:** natural grass, patchy green, with more than one football pitch. The Football panorama is a drone shot over the infield. It shows the sandy track ringing a grass field with several full-size and small training goals, all white [verified].
- **Hockey:** a separate floodlit grass field with hockey goals. A green-netted enclosure (probably cricket practice nets [visual estimate]) sits on its far side, and chain-link fences on concrete posts divide it from the next fields [verified, Hockey panorama].
- **Floodlights:** many tall slim steel poles, each topped with a cluster of LED floodlight heads. They run all round the track and the hockey field, roughly every 40–60 m [visual estimate]. The night panoramas (Football, Hockey, Cricket) show them all lit [verified]. Pole height is about 15–20 m [visual estimate].
- **Edges:** a low cream/beige boundary wall about 1–1.2 m high with a pipe railing or chain-link fence on top, then a row of large neem/kikar-type trees [verified, Athletics and Skating panoramas; species are a visual estimate].
- **Small buildings:**
  - Several single-storey cream-plastered store or pavilion rooms with flat roofs at the field edge [verified, Athletics].
  - A long single-storey cream building with a pitched roof and a row of openings [verified, Athletics; its function is unknown].
  - A two-storey cream building with a gable end, behind the basketball stands [verified, Basketball panorama; its name is unknown].
- **Stands:** long low tiers of blue-painted concrete steps (about 4–6 rows) along the field and courts [verified, Athletics and Basketball].

### C/D/E. Floors, stairs, interior
This is an open ground: no floors, stairs or interior. Stand tiers are about 0.4 m rise × 0.6–0.7 m tread [visual estimate].

## H. Comparison with the game today

**Game today** [verified, `buildGymkhana()` and the screenshots]:
- The ground is a flat green area with a dirt-coloured oval (the `track` material is already sandy, which is correct).
- There is one football field (60 × 96 m) with thin white lines and two thin white goals.
- The courts are plain blue flat rectangles, with no lines, hoops, nets, fences or stands.
- The pool is a flat light-blue quad on bare earth.
- Med-C ground is a plain green polygon.
- There are no floodlights, stands, boundary wall or sports buildings.

**What matches:** the polygon positions, the unpaved track, the grass infield and the blue court colour [verified].

**Ranked fixes:**
1. **Floodlight poles** (15–20 m, LED clusters) around the track, the hockey field, the courts and the cricket ground. They are the most visible silhouette, and they should glow at night.
2. **Court markings and furniture:** basketball keys in maroon/red with a green surround, hoops; tennis lines and nets; volleyball changed to a **sand** surface with nets inside a chain-link cage.
3. **Blue concrete stands** with a jaali parapet between the basketball and tennis blocks, and along the field.
4. **Pool:** a 25 × 12.5 m water rectangle sunk into a pale tiled deck, with a wall or fence and a cream changing block, inside the OSM polygon. Today it is an oversized blue quad.
5. **Med-C cricket ground:** a central circular bare clay pitch patch, stumps and floodlights.
6. **Low cream boundary walls** with pipe railings around the ground and courts, and chain-link fences between fields.
7. **Skating rink:** a grey tiled slab of about 30 × 60 m near (364, 375), with a pipe railing.
8. **More goals:** extra football and hockey goals, and a hockey field with a green practice-net enclosure.
9. Optional: a closed indoor sports hall with an aqua and maroon-truss interior once its location is confirmed.
